A selection of the dashboards I use to follow rates and credit, real estate, gold and digital-asset cash flows. They bring the public data I track into one place and present it visually, so conditions can be read at a glance. The snapshot refreshes daily.

Dashboards

About this project

Purpose

The dashboards keep the conditions behind credit and real-estate risk in one view: the level and shape of rates and spreads, the direction of growth and the labor market, and property values, rents and yields down to the San Francisco ZIP-code level. The gold and digital-asset dashboards apply the same framework to supply and demand, ownership and earnings.

Method

Each dashboard is a Python module that fetches and caches its sources, runs the calculations (index rebasing, rolling betas, yield and multiple math, concentration indices, supply/demand balances), and returns Plotly figures, tables or written reads through FastAPI. The same backend originally powered these dashboards inside OpenBB Workspace. A build script snapshots every tile into this static site, checks each one for errors and keeps the last good version if a source fails.

Data and freshness

All data is public: FRED, the CFTC, SEC 13F filings, Zillow, Redfin, the World Gold Council, LBMA, DefiLlama and others, credited on each dashboard. The snapshot rebuilds daily. Each series updates on its own source's schedule, so monthly and quarterly data will lag the snapshot date. A tile marked stale means its latest refresh failed and it is showing the previous good snapshot.

Data comes from the public sources credited on each dashboard and remains subject to those providers' terms. Shown for research and educational purposes only. Nothing here is investment advice.
